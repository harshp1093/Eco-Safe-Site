Who we are

A Zambian company on a mission to replace single-use plastic.

Eco Safe Zambia is a Zambian company dedicated to biodegradable tableware — sourcing, supplying and promoting alternatives to plastic and polystyrene for restaurants, caterers, events, shops and homes across the country.

Biodegradable

Made from plant-based kraft paper that breaks down naturally instead of lingering for centuries.

Safe for food

Clean, sturdy packaging for hot and cold meals — no compromise on quality or hygiene.

Rooted in Zambia

A local business serving local customers, helping keep our cities, rivers and parks clean.

Our brand

Meet Kind Earth.

Kind Earth is Eco Safe Zambia's own brand of kraft paper packaging. Natural, unbleached-look paper products designed for takeaways, cafés, events and everyday use — packaging you can feel good about handing to your customers.

Why it matters

Protecting Zambia's wild places.

Plastic waste doesn't stay where it's dropped — it travels into our rivers, wetlands and national parks. Every plastic plate replaced with Kind Earth kraft paper is one less piece of litter threatening the wildlife and biodiversity that make Zambia extraordinary.
